Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register for IEC JKUAT membership, we collect: your full name, student registration number, college, course, year of study, phone number, and JKUAT student email address. When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: process your membership registration, communicate about club events and activities, send newsletters (if subscribed), and improve our programs. We do not share your personal data with third parties except as necessary for payment processing via M-Pesa.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in our Supabase database. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information.

4. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at info@iecjkuat.com.

5. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, reach us at: JKUAT Main Campus, Student Center Room 201, or email info@iecjkuat.com.
