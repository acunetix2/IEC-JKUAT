What is IEC?

A student movement. Not a lecture.

IEC JKUAT isn't a class you sit in. It's a space where students from every discipline at JKUAT come together to experiment, build things, solve real problems and grow beyond their degree.

Whether you have a startup idea, a skill you want to apply, or you're still figuring out what you're about — there's a place for you here.

The IEC journey

From idea to impact.

01

Discover

Find problems worth solving. Ask questions. Look around. The best ideas start with noticing something that doesn't work.

02

Create

Sketch it out. Brainstorm with others. Shape a vague idea into something that has a direction.

03

Build

Work with a team. Make something tangible. A working prototype beats a perfect plan every time.

04

Test

Get feedback from real users. Break it. Fix it. Learn more in a week of testing than a semester of theory.

05

Launch

Take your project beyond the classroom. Into the real world. Into the hands of the people it was built for.
